# Privacy Policy – ExTracky

**Effective Date:** November 26, 2026

ExTracky ("the App") is an offline-first expense tracking application developed by Vocals Local. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

## 1. Information We Store

ExTracky allows you to record and manage personal expenses, including:

- Expense titles and amounts
- Expense categories
- Transaction dates
- Optional notes

This information is stored locally on your device using its local database. The App does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use its core features.

## 2. Data Collection and Sharing

ExTracky does not operate its own server or transmit your expense records to us.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal expense information with third parties.

The App does not use advertising or analytics services.

## 3. Data Export

ExTracky provides an option to export expense records as a CSV file.

When you choose to export or share this file, it is handled through your device's sharing functionality. Any information you choose to share may be received or processed by the application or service you select. Their privacy policies will apply.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

Your expense records and app preferences are stored locally on your device.

We recommend keeping your device secure and maintaining appropriate backups of any exported information you wish to retain.

## 5. Data Deletion

You can delete individual expense records or clear all expense records using the options available in the App.

Uninstalling the App generally removes its locally stored application data, subject to your device's backup and restoration settings.

## 6. Children's Privacy

ExTracky is a general-purpose expense tracking application. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our own servers.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features or privacy practices change. Any updated version will be published at the privacy policy URL, with a revised effective date.
